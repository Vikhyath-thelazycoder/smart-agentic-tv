# Jarvis

Jarvis lives on the TV. You can reach it from the **Jarvis** tab, **Say a command** on the Remote tab, and the mic on the Shows tab.

Simple commands like "volume up" or "open YouTube" work by plain rules, with no AI and no key.

## Things to say

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "What is the capital of Japan?" then "what about its population?" | Answers, and keeps the conversation for 10 minutes |
| "Play Kantara" | Finds the title and the app, then opens it |
| "In Zee5 open Ayyana Mane" | Opens it in that app, or searches there |
| "On YouTube search Kantara trailer" | Shows YouTube's results |
| "Something funny for tonight" | Suggestions, with the app for each |
| "Continue watching" | Reopens the last title opened from the phone |
| "What's on my screen?" | Describes a picture of the screen |
| "Remind me in 10 minutes to check the food" | Shows and says it on the TV |
| "Set a timer for 5 minutes", "cancel timers" | Timers on the TV |
| "Turn off the TV in 30 minutes" | Warns a minute before |
| "When I say movie night, set the volume to 30 and open YouTube" | Saves a routine; say "movie night" to run it |

You can speak English, Hindi or Kannada, using the language button at the top. Replies are in English. The menu chooses where they're spoken: phone, TV or nowhere.

The TV's sound drops while you talk, and comes back afterwards.

## How it stays safe

- The AI only proposes actions from a fixed list. Anything else is dropped.
- Turning the TV off, deleting a routine or removing a saved title waits for your yes. That yes is checked by plain code, so the AI can't approve its own action.
- Reading the screen can't trigger actions. Text in the picture only comes back as words.
- What it tells you after acting comes from what really happened.
- Factory apps listed in the board profile can't be opened.

## Limits

- "Continue watching" only knows titles opened from the phone. Apps don't share their history.
- Some apps block pictures of their screen, and Jarvis will say so.
- Reminders only ring while the TV is on. One missed by more than an hour is dropped.
- Some boards power fully off, so only the TV's own remote can turn them back on.
