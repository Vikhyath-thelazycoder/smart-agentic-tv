# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Please:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give useful feedback, and remember many people here are new to this.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sharing someone's private information, or repeatedly disturbing a discussion.

Maintainers may remove content or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
