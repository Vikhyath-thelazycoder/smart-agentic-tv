# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- New home screen for Android TV boards, built from Free My TV
- Phone remote: pad, mouse, typing, shows search, voice commands
- Jarvis assistant on Gemini and Groq, with timers, routines and "what's on my screen"
- Your own API keys, kept on the TV
- Board profiles, starting with the U.11 KitKing board
- Phones can get the app from a link on the TV
